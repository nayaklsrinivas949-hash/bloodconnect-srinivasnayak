Complete Healthcare Ecosystem

Built for Hospitals, Designed for Donors

A unified dual-persona platform ensuring rapid emergency dispatch, verifiable medical fitness, and real rewards.

🛰️

3D Proximity Radar

Hospitals set a dynamic distance radius (1km to 100km). The 3D holographic radar calculates Haversine GPS proximity and highlights active compatible donors with laser trajectories.

🏥

Hospital Admin Dispatch

Post emergency patient blood requests with urgency levels (Critical / High / Standard). Send instant SOS broadcasts to matching nearby donors and track real-time responses.

🧬

Daily Medical Fitness Log

Donors update daily health metrics (sleep, hydration, vitals). The system enforces the 56-day whole-blood cooldown timer and computes a real-time Medical Readiness Score.

📋

Hospital Visit Verification

When a donor visits the hospital, the admin verifies their digital pass, logs donation parameters (Hb, BP, units), resets the cooldown, and credits rewards instantly.

🪙

Medical Points Redemption

Earn 500 points per donation + streak bonuses. Redeem points for real health benefits: free full-body tests, 25% pharmacy discount vouchers, and family blood guarantee tokens.

🔒

Privacy & Encrypted Passes

Donor personal information is safeguarded with consent-based dispatch passes and digital encrypted QR codes for seamless check-in at partner hospitals.
